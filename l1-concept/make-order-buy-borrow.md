# Make / Order / Buy / Borrow

## Recommendation

Buy or borrow rated grips. Make only adapters and non-load-bearing setup aids after the machine interface and load range are known.

## Buy

- Wedge, collet, threaded, or button-head grips with manufacturer capacity ratings.
- Replacement jaws, inserts, pins, and retainers for the selected grip model.
- Manufacturer-compatible guards or enclosure parts when available.

## Make

- Machine-specific adapters between the load frame and rated grips.
- Alignment tools for setup checks.
- Shield mounts that do not enter the primary load path.
- Storage trays or labeled setup blocks for jaws, pins, and specimen types.

## Order

- Traceable 4140 prehard, 17-4PH, or engineer-approved metal stock for adapters.
- Rated fasteners with known grade and certification when they enter the load path.
- Clear guarding material only after thickness, mounting, and impact assumptions are reviewed.

## Borrow

- Certified grips from a university, test lab, or vendor demo kit for the first fixture validation.
- Load cells matched to lower-force specimens if the available load cell is too large for good resolution or too close to overload.

## Do Not Use

- Unknown scrap in the primary load path.
- 3D-printed plastic grips or adapters for metallic tensile fracture tests.
- Unrated clamps, vises, or improvised jaw stacks.
- Shielding whose material, condition, or mounting method is unknown.
