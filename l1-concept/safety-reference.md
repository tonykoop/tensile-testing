# Safety and Welfare Reference

This file is the required safety/welfare reference for the L1 concept packet. It is a checklist of authorities and controls to verify before this concept becomes a physical fixture or test procedure.

## References to Verify

- ASTM E8/E8M, current edition: specimen geometry, strain measurement, reporting, and metallic tension-test method requirements.
- Universal testing machine manufacturer manual: frame capacity, controller behavior, emergency stop, crosshead limits, guarding, and approved accessories.
- Grip manufacturer manual: rated capacity, accepted specimen end geometry, jaw installation, lubrication limits, and proof/inspection requirements.
- OSHA 29 CFR 1910 Subpart O / 1910.212 machine-guarding principles, or the local jurisdiction equivalent, before any powered shop or lab use.
- Local shop safety rules for PPE, machine authorization, guarding, training, and incident reporting.

## Welfare Scope

The protected parties are operators, observers, shop staff, and anyone near the test frame. No animal welfare or human-subject testing is part of this repository.

## Stored-Energy Controls

- Keep people out of the fracture plane.
- Prefer a rated enclosure or manufacturer guard over improvised shields.
- Treat clear shielding as a rated safety component, not as a visibility convenience.
- Review fragment, jaw, pin, and adapter ejection paths before each setup.
- Do not run unattended fracture tests.

## Required Review Before Build

- Mechanical calculation for expected peak load and controlling component.
- Material and heat-treatment record for any custom adapter.
- Drawing review for stress concentrations, thread engagement, pin shear, and bearing area.
- Safety review for shielding, operator position, and emergency stop access.
- Proof or low-load checkout plan that does not intentionally fracture a specimen.

## Non-Negotiable L1 Boundary

This packet is not a certified test method, not an ASTM compliance claim, and not a substitute for the current standards, machine manuals, or a qualified shop safety review.
