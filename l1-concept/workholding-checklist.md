# Workholding Checklist

## Datums

- Specimen axis is the primary datum.
- Grip centerlines must coincide with the load-frame centerline.
- Adapter faces must seat squarely without rocking.
- Pins, shoulders, or threads must bottom or seat only where intended.

## Clamping and Retention

- Use rated grips matched to specimen end geometry.
- Confirm jaw faces, collets, pins, or threaded ends are clean and undamaged.
- Apply grip preload consistently and per manufacturer instructions.
- Do not rely on friction-only retention for a custom jaw unless it has been engineered and proof tested.

## Tool Access

- Operator must be able to install the specimen without placing hands in a powered pinch zone.
- Wrenches and setup tools must clear the shield and frame columns.
- Extensometer or strain fixture access must not compromise shielding.

## Inspection Points

- Verify specimen diameter and gage length before setup.
- Inspect adapter threads, pins, shoulders, and fasteners before each session.
- Check for bending, jaw slip, or asymmetric witness marks after preload.
- Stop if the load trace shows slipping, chatter, or unexpected compliance.

## Failure Watch Items

- Specimen half ejection after fracture.
- Jaw insert kick-out.
- Adapter thread pullout.
- Pin shear or pin walkout.
- Shield contact from fragments or whipping specimen halves.
